## N Songs

There are 7 possibilities: N = 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 songs.

If there are 6 or more songs, the route will basically be to advance the Trade Quest until you reach the Poacher’s Saw.

- Odd Potion RBA: Song of Time (Storms Deletes)

- Saw RBA: Zelda's Lullaby, Serenade, Nocturne, Prelude (Requiem, Epona, Saria, Sun's Song deletes)

- Broken Goron Sword RBA: (Minuet Bolero deletes)

The basic route for the 10 songs (Sun’s and Requiem are in no particular order):\
Odd Potion RBA → Storms → Saw RBA → Minuet → Bolero → Sun’s → Requiem\
. For songs 8 and 9, skip Sun’s and Requiem.\
For songs 6 and 7, skip Storms.

Since Saria and Epona are deleted with Saw RBA, there is basically no point in taking them, but if you clear the Forest Temple and take the route that goes back in time, it is possible to go and retrieve Saria and Epona after the Saw RBA.

In the case of 3 or 4 songs, if you collect Minuet, Bolero, Storms (Sun, Requiem, Serenade), you can do it even if you don't have to use the Trade Quest Route. If you don't do the Saw RBA, you can also use Saria and Epona.

## 5 Top Row Songs

New goals added in v10.5

5 out of 6: Zelda's Lullaby, Epona's Song, Saria's Song, Sun's Song, Song of Time, Song of Storms

The Storms will be deleted with odd potion RBA, and Epona, Saria, and the Sun’s Song will be deleted with the saw RBA, so be careful.

Basic route:\
Go to Castle Town as a child and wake up Talon → Zelda's Lullaby → Save Reset → Saria's Song → Epona's Song → Sun's Song + Song of Storms after becoming an adult

If there are other benefits to returning to child, you can go and get Saria or Epona after the Saw RBA.\
Odd Potion RBA (Song of Time) → Song of Storms → Saw RBA (Zelda's Lullaby) → Sun's Song → Clear the Forest Temple and return to child → Wake Talon → Epona's Song

# Boss Key goal

## N Boss Keys

N = 2,3

If there are no other goals to balance, the basics are fire and water (Ganon's Castle)

- Forest Temple: After the block puzzle, shoot the eye switch to twist the corridor. You can get a bow in the Stalfos room at the back, but if you have an Arrow Holder (or Ruto's Letter RBA ) + Ice Arrows (or Equip Swap the Slingshot) you can save a key and avoid fighting the Stalfos. You can also get them with the Coupon RBA .

- Fire Temple: First door on the left when you enter the temple. Use weirdshot to get it. Can also be obtained with Fire Arrows RBA.

- Water Temple: Ice Arrows Obtained through RBA. Not obtained through the regular method.

- Shadow Temple: You almost need Lullaby. Take the boat and go through the invisible wall maze (the door at the back right of the maze). [<u>Din's Flame is not necessary</u>](https://youtu.be/9QkP__Qlano) . It is not realistic to do it [<u>without Lullaby .</u>](https://youtu.be/Esu4gNRsp24)

- Spirit Temple: Requires Lullaby. After obtaining the Mirror Shield, climb the moving wall to the room ahead. You can also use [<u>Death Hookshot Jump</u>](https://youtu.be/Cave9bsiiJw) from the room below . You can also get the Light Arrows RBA.

- Ganon's Castle: You need a way to get into Ganon's Castle ( [<u>Bombchu Hover</u>](https://youtu.be/L5PbgPlfT04) or [<u>Ganondoor</u>](https://youtu.be/X4P4VUHEn8g) , although it's difficult, you can also use [<u>HESS HoverSlide</u>](https://youtu.be/1EktAQZz7tc?si=5j9RzPqkB79TOYpW) ). The key is located after the Stalfos fight inside the tower, so if you use Ganondoor, you don't need to skip the Trial.

- Deku Dodongo Jabu: Can be obtained through Prescription RBA, Diamond Frog RBA, and Eye Drops RBA respectively, but are almost never used.

# Equipment goals

## 3 Swords

The Giant's Knife costs 200 rupees, so you'll need a wallet,\
which you can get by using the Cucco RBA or by catching 10 Skulltulas.

You can buy the knife from Chugoron behind the bomb wall in Goron City. The Biggoron Sword will work, but in most cases the knife is faster.\
If you want to get both the Giant's Knife and the Goron's Clothes, you can use the Return A technique to buy the Giant's Knife for free.\
(Note that you need to have 200 rupees to buy it)\
[<u>https://youtu.be/3H960MGHtSY</u>](https://youtu.be/3H960MGHtSY)

The Kokiri Sword can be obtained in Kokiri Forest. If you use blank A or skull push to do a well kiss, it is easy to skip it, so be careful not to forget.\
If you turn from an adult back into a child, the sword will be set to the B button even if you haven't picked it up, but it won't fill up your item list and won't count as having been acquired, so be careful.

The Master Sword is obtained when you become an adult.

## 3 Shields

To get to the Mirror Shield, you can either use the Spirit Hover or go inside the Spirit Temple.\
You'll need a Bombchu to go to the Spirit Hover. It's easier to get inside if you have Zelda's Lullaby.\
Even if you don't have Zelda's Lullaby, you can get to the Silver Gauntlets chest by using Damage Ground Clip twice from the room in front of the Iron Knuckle (on the child's side), and then use HoverSlide to get it.

The Deku Shield is as follows:

- Purchased in Kokiri Forest (40 rupees)

- Treasure chest at the bottom of the well (Fireke and Beamos room)

- Dodongo's Cavern Akindonut (50 rupees)

- Dodongo's Cavern chest (opposite the bomb bag after a doom jump)

- Spirit Temple chest (comes with Bombchus)

The Hylian Shield can be purchased from the store (80 rupees) or obtained from a chest at the bottom of the well in the Graveyard (in the room with Like-Like and Gold Sta).

## 3 Tunics

Goron Tunic can be obtained by blowing up the rolling Goron or by buying it from the Goron Shop (200 rupees). You will need a wallet to buy it.

# Dungeon Item goals

## N Compasses

There are four possibilities: N = 4, 5, 6, and 7.

The compass cannot be obtained in RBA. If you try to get the map or boss key of the same dungeon in RBA after obtaining the compass, the compass will disappear.

I'll probably go for Well, Shadow, and Dodongo. Next are Deku, Water, Ice, and Jabu.\
Forest, Fire, and Spirit take time, so it depends on other goals.

- Deku Tree: 3rd floor room

- Dodongo's Cavern: Destroy the bomb wall near the bomb flower stairs.

- Jabu Jabu: Beyond the red blob in the maze at the back. There are a lot of splatters, so it's a good idea to have some Deku Nuts. You can get in with [<u>a HoverSlide , so you don't need a boomerang.</u>](https://youtu.be/3UukSS-rAjw)

- Well: Central room. Enter through the secret passage in the left wall.
